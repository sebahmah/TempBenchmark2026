Period: 1 Jun – 30 Sep 2026, 30-minute readings from the five BMS exports. Missing readings are skipped, not filled.

Portfolio average: simple mean of the five foodcourt averages, so a foodcourt with five sensors does not outweigh one with four.

Group percentiles: for each day, P10, P50 and P90 of all readings from all sensors in all foodcourts that day.

In band: a day counts when the foodcourt's daily average is between that day's group P10 and P90, inclusive.
